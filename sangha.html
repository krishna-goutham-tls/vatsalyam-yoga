Wellness Sangha

A supportive community for women who wish to prioritize their well-being amidst the demands of everyday life.

Through simple, sustainable practices, expert-led sessions, meaningful conversations, and a nurturing peer community, the Sangha helps women build healthier habits, manage stress, improve emotional well-being, and reconnect with themselves.

What You’ll Experience

  • Guided wellness practices and habit-building
  • Expert sessions on health, mindset and emotional well-being
  • A supportive community of like-minded women
  • Practical tools to create balance, energy and resilience in daily life
