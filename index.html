About Vatsalyam Yoga

The word “Vatsalyam” represents the affection and nurturing care of a mother. That spirit forms the foundation of everything we do.

Vatsalyam Yoga is a wellness space created to support physical, mental and emotional well-being through movement, breath, mindfulness and self-awareness. The intention is to help people feel seen, supported and encouraged to grow at their own pace.

Whether someone joins to improve physical health, manage stress, regain confidence in movement or simply reconnect with themselves, the practice is designed to support everyday life — not just time spent on the mat.

Classes & Offerings

Every body is different. Every life stage is different. Yoga at Vatsalyam is adapted to the individual — not the other way around. Each class is offered online and in Bangalore, with small groups either way.

Women's Yoga

Strength, mobility, flexibility, balance, breath awareness, stress management, perimenopause support and sustainable wellness habits.

Senior Citizens Yoga

Gentle practices for mobility, balance, breathing, confidence, independence and community.

Pranayama & Meditation

Personalized support for knee care, spine care, women's wellness, safe movement and therapeutic practice.

Meet the Founder

Rajeswari Sitaraman

Fondly known as Raji. Yoga educator, wellness advocate and former IT professional.

For nearly two decades, she worked in the corporate IT industry, navigating deadlines, responsibilities, long hours and the constant balancing act between professional and personal life. Yoga entered her life not merely as exercise, but as a powerful tool for self-discovery, healing and transformation.

  • M.Sc. (Information Systems), BITS Pilani
  • M.Sc. Yoga
  • Nearly 20 years in Information Technology
  • Former Tata Consultancy Services professional
  • Teacher Training in Yoga Therapy for Women
  • Applied Anatomy in Yoga Teaching
  • Ongoing study of Pranayama, Meditation and Therapeutic Applications of Yoga
  • Certified Kids Yoga Teacher

What Grounds Me

Yoga has transformed the way I relate to my body and mind. Equally, my ongoing study of Vedanta (Bhagavad Gita and Upanishads) under the guidance of my teacher has transformed the way I understand life itself.

These teachings continue to provide grounding, clarity, perspective and inner balance. They remind me that true well-being is not merely physical fitness, but also cultivating a calmer mind, greater self-awareness and a deeper connection with oneself.

This understanding naturally informs the way I teach, live and engage with the people I work with.
